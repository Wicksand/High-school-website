## Team Collaboration Schedule

As this is a university project, the team meets every Friday to collaborate on the project throughout development. These meetings provide time to review progress, discuss open questions, and ensure the project stays aligned with client needs and course requirements.

## Development Focus by Phase

### Phase 1: Foundation and Planning
- review client needs and project goals
- clarify target audiences and required product features
- establish initial front-end design concepts

### Phase 2: Core System Design
- define registration flow
- begin structure for schedule display
- plan merchandise reservation workflow
- outline administrative content management needs

### Phase 3: Build and Refinement
- implement website front-end structure and styling
- integrate functional elements for registration and reservations
- refine information architecture and usability

### Phase 4: Final Preparation
- polish site content and design
- verify project requirements against the client brief
- prepare for milestone presentations and final delivery

## Project Intent

This schedule supports a steady development process where design, functionality, and user experience continue to improve with each module. It also creates clear checkpoints for team review and client-facing demonstration before final delivery.

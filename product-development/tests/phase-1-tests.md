## Feedback: Standard Website

- Emily: Keep the navigation bar visible while scrolling. Marked the standard design as preferred.
- Stacie: Show what merchandise is available, keep the top menu navigation, remove the form from the bottom, and preserve the user-friendly navigation and easy-to-find information. Marked the standard design as preferred.
- Elizabeth: Likes that the site is straightforward and short; some tab destinations should be different.
- Luke: Likes the organization and colors. If other sports are added, consider a top dropdown that switches the page to the selected sport.

## Feedback: Mobile App Website

- Emily: Move registration higher, adjust the green-looking top color, make the site more cohesive, and keep the bottom mobile navigation bar.
- Stacie: Show what merchandise is available.
- Elizabeth: The page is too long, but the All / High School / Grid Kids tabs and their functionality are strong.
- Luke: Reduce the side margins, update the hero banner color, keep the blue organization, clarify which schools Grid Kids serves, and avoid mentioning only the high school when describing the program.

## Initial Findings

- The standard website is currently preferred by two testers and is viewed as straightforward and compact.
- Both designs need clearer merchandise visibility.
- Persistent navigation is valuable, but the preferred navigation pattern differs by context: top navigation for the standard design and bottom navigation for mobile.
- The mobile design needs a shorter page and clearer Grid Kids coverage.
- The project should clarify whether support for additional sports is in scope before adding a sport-selection control.

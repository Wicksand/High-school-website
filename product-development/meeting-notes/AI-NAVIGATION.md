# AI Navigation Guide for Meeting Notes

This folder contains notes from meetings about the Thunderridge High School Football Booster Club website.

## How to Use This Folder

1. Read the relevant meeting note before making assumptions about client needs, program scope, branding, or workflows.
2. Use the meeting date and note title to determine which information is newest.
3. Treat statements about client decisions as evidence from that meeting, not as a replacement for later confirmed decisions.
4. Preserve uncertainty. Phrases such as “maybe,” “to be determined,” or “will have to double check” indicate open questions.
5. Compare meeting notes with `../about-product/about-product.md`, `../site-requirements/site-requirements.md`, and `../product-schedule/product-schedule.md` when requirements or priorities are involved.
6. Record new meeting notes as separate Markdown files rather than rewriting older notes.

## Current Notes

- `m1.md` contains the first meeting notes from September 29, 2026.

## What to Extract

When summarizing or implementing from meeting notes, look for:

- client decisions and requests
- program or audience definitions
- registration, scheduling, and merchandise workflows
- content and branding sources
- open questions and items awaiting client confirmation
- decisions that affect a particular demo or project phase

Meeting notes are contextual evidence. They should be used with the formal requirements and schedule, not as a substitute for either document.

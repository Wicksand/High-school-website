# Demo 2: Mobile-First App-Style Experience

## Design Direction

Design this demo to feel more like a simple mobile app than a traditional school website. Prioritize a clear, compact experience that works especially well on phones and makes common tasks quick to find.

## Demo Purpose

Present an alternative concept for the Thunderridge High School Football Booster Club website. Explore how a mobile-first, app-style layout could make schedules, registration, and merchandise reservations easy to access for families and supporters.

## Project Context

The overall project serves students, families, boosters, contributors, and school district members. It is expected to support Thunderridge High School Football (grades 9-12) and Thunderridge Grid Kids Football (grades 4-8).

Known core needs include:

- sharing schedules and calendars
- making registration easier to find and complete
- allowing merchandise reservations without online transactions
- providing a way for authorized administrators to update site information

## Design Principles

- Start with phone-sized screens and scale up for tablets and desktop.
- Keep navigation and primary actions simple and immediately accessible.
- Present schedules and important updates in a compact, scannable format.
- Clearly distinguish high school and Grid Kids information where needed.
- Keep merchandise reservations clearly separate from payments or checkout.
- Treat this document as a working brief; revise it when the team supplies additional references and preferences.

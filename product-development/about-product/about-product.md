# About the Product

## Project Overview

This project is a real-world website created by a three-person student team in the course at https://byui-cse.github.io/wdd-x99-course/. The team consists of Joshua, Samantha, and Andrew.

The product is a website for the Thunderridge High School Football Booster Club. It will serve as a central hub for information, communication, and event-related tools for football activities at Thunderridge High School and the associated Grid Kids football program.

## Primary Purpose

The website is intended to serve as a digital home for:

- students and families
- parents and guardians
- boosters and contributors
- school district members
- youth football participants and their families

It will help users stay informed about football-related events, schedules, registrations, merchandise opportunities, and organizational updates.

## Target Scope

The site will cover information related to:

- Thunderridge High School Football (grades 9-12)
- Thunderridge Grid Kids Football (grades 4-8)

There are currently two football teams for each grade level, which results in a total of 18 teams across the program. Because of this structure, the site needs to be organized in a way that is easy to navigate for multiple age groups, programs, and family types.

## Client and Stakeholder Context

This project is being developed for a real client representing the Thunderridge High School Football Booster Club. The relationship includes coordination with the organization behind the football programs and the associated youth group.

The client has indicated that the new website should make critical tasks easier and more streamlined for families and organizers, especially around registration, scheduling, and merchandise reservations.

## What the Website Needs to Do

The site should simplify key operations that are currently fragmented or handled through informal channels. The main areas of focus are:

1. Registration
   - Create a clearer and more modern registration experience
   - Move away from dependence on Facebook-based handling
   - Plan for registration submissions to route to email or another centralized system

2. Merchandise Reservation
   - Build a reservation system from the ground up
   - Keep the process separate from payment or transactional checkout
   - Allow families to reserve items without completing a financial transaction on the site

3. Schedules and Calendars
   - Provide a clear way to share team schedules and upcoming events
   - Keep information visible and easy to update

4. Content Sharing
   - Publish club information, announcements, and social content from channels such as Facebook and Instagram
   - Create a more organized and professional public-facing experience

5. Program Coordination
   - Support both the high school football organization and the Grid Kids football program
   - Potentially connect or align with existing program resources where appropriate

## Current Content and Integration Considerations

The client already has social media content that can be used as a source for current information. The Facebook and Instagram pages are considered valid sources for public-facing content and should be incorporated thoughtfully into the website strategy.

There is also an existing Grid Kids website, https://teamsideline.com/ThunderRidgeGridKids, which may need to be merged into or coordinated with the new website. The final direction for that integration is still to be determined, but the need to unify or support both audiences is clear.

## Project Goals

The core goals for the website are:

- make registration easier to manage
- simplify merchandise reservation
- improve schedule accessibility and communication
- centralize football-related information for the organization
- create a polished, user-friendly front-end experience
- give the booster club an admin-friendly way to maintain content

## Design and Product Priorities

The team will focus on the following early priorities:

- developing front-end mockup options for presentation
- designing the systems for registration, schedule management, and merchandise reservations
- creating a clean and welcoming public-facing layout
- preparing a structure that can support future content updates and administration

## Product Direction

The website should feel trustworthy, organized, and community-focused. It should stand as a polished representation of the Thunderridge football program and help bring together the school-level and youth-level football audiences under one clearer digital experience.

This project will act as the baseline for the website development work that follows, including planning, design, and implementation of the final solution.

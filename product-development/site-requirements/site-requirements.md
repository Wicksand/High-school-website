# Site Requirements

## Overview

This site requires the following core capabilities:

- schedules and calendars
- registration functionality
- merchandise reservation functionality
- an admin page to update content and information across the website

These features form the foundation of the user experience and the administrative workflow for the Thunder Ridge Football Booster Club website.

## Functional Requirements

### 1. Schedule and Calendar Features
The website must provide a way to share team schedules and event information in a clear and easy-to-read format.

Requirements:

- display schedule information for football teams and events
- include calendar-style or schedule-style viewing for users
- make upcoming events visible to families and supporters
- support updates from administrators when schedules change

### 2. Registration Functionality
The website must provide a registration system that is easier and more professional than the existing Facebook-based process.

Requirements:

- allow users to register for football-related activities
- collect the required participant information in a consistent format
- direct registration submissions to a structured email or central tracking process
- keep the process simple enough for parents and guardians to complete successfully

### 3. Merchandise Reservation System
The site must include a merchandise reservation feature that allows users to request items without completing a transaction.

Requirements:

- provide a reservation form for merchandise items
- allow users to select desired products or categories
- store or route reservation requests for processing
- ensure the feature is clearly labeled as reservation-only, not a direct checkout system

### 4. Administrative Content Management
The site must include an administrator page or backend-style interface for updating website content.

Requirements:

- allow authorized users to edit content after launch
- update schedules, club information, and announcements
- manage promotional or informational content without needing a developer for each change
- keep the admin experience simple and organized

### 5. Public Information and Community Content
The site should provide a polished presentation of the club and its programs.

Requirements:

- present official club information clearly
- connect or summarize content from existing social media channels
- support communication with families and supporters
- represent the school and youth football programs in a professional way

## Non-Functional Requirements

### Usability
- the site should be easy for parents, students, and contributors to navigate
- important actions such as registration and reservation should be easy to find
- the layout should be clean, welcoming, and mobile-friendly

### Maintainability
- content should be easy to update as schedules and events change
- the site structure should support future expansion as the program grows

### Security and Privacy
- registration forms should be handled carefully and securely
- access to admin tools should be restricted to authorized users
- sensitive participant information should be handled responsibly

### Consistency
- branding, language, and layout should remain consistent across the site
- design should reflect a dependable and organized booster club presence

## Business and Program Rules

- registration is a core operational need and should be prioritized in the experience
- merchandise reservations are informational and logistical, not transactional
- the Grid Kids program may require a separate account or workflow from the high school football booster account
- content from existing Facebook and Instagram pages may be used as a foundation for updated site information
- the final product should support both the Thunder Ridge High School program and the Grid Kids football audience

## Acceptance Criteria

The site will be considered successful when it includes:

- a clear public-facing home or information page for the Booster Club
- schedule and calendar functionality that is easy to maintain
- a registration process for users to complete their needed forms
- a merchandise reservation workflow that does not function as a payment platform
- an admin interface for updates and content management
- a design that supports the needs of the football community and the school organization

## Priority Direction

The most immediate priorities are the registration system, schedule communication, and merchandise reservation process. These areas directly support the everyday operations of the booster club and represent the highest-value site functionality.

## Test Record

The following feedback is from the five MVP version 1 entries in the testing sheet. The source does not record specific tasks attempted or tester-by-tester follow-up status.

| Tester | Demographic role | Feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stacie Wicks | Parent/Guardian | Likes the progress and functionality. A storefront and a missing block are still needed, especially on mobile; clarify where that menu option will go. Replace the filler events. Consider displaying the boxes in individual columns on mobile. |
| Emily Wicks | Student/Player | Likes the color changes and the different text colorations. |
| Aubry Wicks | Educator | Add a link to follow on social media. Make registration links open in a new tab. Has mixed feelings about the lower menu buttons on mobile. |
| Paige Rubio | Parent/Guardian | Likes the overall display, but wants more pictures and distinctive details, such as a logo or mascot, to set the site apart from other high schools. |
| Joanna Merrell | Parent/Guardian | Make the schedule more engaging and, if appropriate, add another color for variety. |

## Findings

- Testers responded positively to the site's progress, functionality, colors, and overall display.
- Feedback identified content and branding needs: replace filler events, add a storefront and social link, and use more photos and distinctive club or school branding.
- Mobile layout needs further review, including the storefront/menu placement, lower menu buttons, and how content boxes are arranged.
- Registration link behavior and schedule presentation were also raised for consideration.

## Follow-Up

The source sheet records feedback but does not indicate which changes have been completed. Confirm and track follow-up decisions with the team before treating any item as resolved.
